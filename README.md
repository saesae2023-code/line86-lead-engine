# Line 86 Lead Engine

Internal lead discovery and qualification engine for Line 86.

## Goal

Find companies that fit the Line 86 ICP, enrich them with public business/contact data, score them, deduplicate them, and pass qualified leads to the Line 86 CRM.

## MVP flow

Discovery → Enrichment → Qualification → Deduplication → CRM

## Initial ICP

Priority segments:
- active Ozon / Wildberries sellers with recurring supply needs;
- wholesalers and online stores;
- manufacturers buying components or goods from China;
- Russian brands sourcing or producing in China;
- businesses that need compliant China → Russia logistics, supplier coordination, QC, labeling, consolidation, or marketplace preparation.

## Lead statuses

- NEW
- QUALIFIED
- HOT
- REJECTED

## MVP principle

Do not maximize raw contact volume. Optimize for qualified commercial opportunities.
